# Seed Build Order V10

## Purpose

This document is the operational handover for Seed 🌱 and the V10 solar electrical topology engine recovery programme.

It preserves the decisions, completed work, repository state, architectural doctrine, ethical constraints, confirmed technical findings and immediate next actions established in the originating thread.

It is an original synthesis, not a verbatim transcript. It does not reproduce private hidden reasoning or copyrighted source material.

## 1. Origin of Seed

The work began as recovery and continuation of the repository:

- <https://github.com/Ventusltd/solar-electrical-topology-analysis-engine-text-based>

The immediate technical problem was that substantial engineering knowledge had accumulated across multiple browser versions, Python modules, JavaScript workbenches, research notes, standards reviews, reports, datasets and separate Ventus repositories.

Continuing linearly from one version risked:

- overlooking stronger logic in older repositories;
- reproducing corrected errors;
- confusing browser behaviour with engineering authority;
- losing provenance;
- repeating investigations;
- creating disconnected tools;
- allowing accumulated knowledge to disappear as software changed.

The recovery programme therefore expanded into the **Matrix Phase**: a disciplined cross-repository harvest of mathematics, engineering logic, schemas, topology, tests, provenance, reports and historical lessons.

The Matrix Phase was then renamed **Seed 🌱** because the aim is not merely to produce a table. The aim is to establish a living knowledge foundation from which future engines, reports, interfaces, datasets and AI systems can grow.

## 2. Seed mission

Seed is intended to become the canonical engineering memory of the Ventus and GlobalGrid2050 ecosystem.

Seed stores:

- original ethical and governance doctrine;
- engineering reasoning;
- context logic;
- formula and physics registers;
- topology and object models;
- evidence and provenance;
- validation and regression history;
- cross-repository capability lineage;
- authority decisions;
- rejected ideas and lessons;
- migration plans;
- reload documents;
- ecosystem links.

Seed is not intended to become:

- a copy of standards;
- a document dump;
- a confidential project archive;
- another isolated application;
- a replacement for primary sources;
- a repository of unattributed third-party code;
- a transcript archive without synthesis.

## 3. Ethical seed

Vikram Kumar supplied an independent distillation of approximately 300 ethical aphorisms drawn from study of the Mahabharata and related traditions.

The distillation emphasised, among other themes:

- dharma is subtle and circumstances affect application;
- truth, welfare and restraint remain central;
- duties can conflict and tragic choices may leave moral debt;
- justice requires courage where power prefers silence;
- guilt belongs to responsible conduct rather than family or community;
- duty must not become blind obedience;
- self-mastery is higher than external victory;
- knowledge requires humility, restraint and practice;
- authority without service becomes tyranny;
- leadership must listen and protect the vulnerable;
- loyalty is bounded by dharma;
- consent and agency cannot be manufactured by rank, fear or custom;
- power requires restraint and accountability;
- time devours power, but truth and deeds may outlast it;
- ecological limits and long-term welfare matter;
- action without attachment still requires wisdom and right judgement.

These principles are used as **governance**, not as technical authority.

They guide how engineering knowledge is selected, communicated and used. They do not determine equations, physical constants, product properties, legal compliance or standards interpretation.

## 4. Copyright and rights doctrine

The user explicitly instructed: **do not violate copyright**.

The governing rule is:

> Seed stores original knowledge, provenance, links and engineering judgement. It does not reproduce copyrighted standards, books, papers, figures, proprietary drawings, confidential reports or substantial protected expression without a clear lawful basis and compatible licence.

Seed may preserve:

- independently written summaries;
- original engineering interpretations;
- independently derived mathematics;
- factual measurements and observations lawfully obtained;
- public identifiers and links;
- clause or section references;
- licence-compatible code with attribution and obligations intact;
- minimal confidential metadata where authorised;
- original abstractions that remove protected or project-specific expression.

Access, ownership of a copy or technical ability to retrieve material does not automatically create publication rights.

For external repositories and forks, Seed must identify upstream provenance and licence before migration.

For private repositories, Seed may inspect when authorised but must not automatically disclose their content publicly.

## 5. Engineering doctrine

The stable engineering order is:

```text
Physical objects
→ geometry
→ terminals and connectivity
→ ordered topology
→ product and conductor properties
→ operating conditions
→ calculations
→ uncertainty
→ validation and compliance layers
→ evidence receipts
→ database aggregation
→ reports
→ visualisation
```

The browser is not authoritative.

Critical engineering mathematics must not exist only inside visual rendering or browser event logic.

The authoritative computation layer should be testable independently of the interface.

The Python physical engine is presently the strongest provisional computation authority.

The V10 JavaScript kernel contains valuable evidence-aware and uncertainty-aware ideas.

The V8 and V9 JavaScript workbenches remain important reference implementations and regression sources.

These authority statements are provisional until the Matrix Phase completes comparative validation.
