## Engineering findings carried into Seed

The public module and inverter evidence changed the required resolution of the model without changing the governing doctrine.

Thirty 660 W modules at the published 45.9 V open-circuit voltage produce 1377 V at standard test conditions. Applying the published open-circuit-voltage temperature coefficient to an illustrative 35 °C reduction gives approximately 1497.5 V. This is a derived screening case near the shared 1500 V ceiling. The exact temperature source, module bin, source revision and tolerance method remain part of the design case rather than universal facts.

Two published module short-circuit currents sum to 36.9 A under front-side standard conditions, 38.74 A at the published five per cent rear-gain case and 40.6 A at the published ten per cent case. The forty-ampere inverter value is described as maximum PV input current, while sixty amperes is the stated short-circuit-current limit per MPPT. The forty-ampere comparison is therefore a screening signal, not yet a final compatibility rule. The exact rating definition and applicable operating-current method must be established.

The published portrait factory leads are 350 mm and 280 mm. Their seventy-millimetre difference becomes 2.1 m across one thirty-module string and 50.4 m across twenty-four strings. Positive and negative factory-lead lengths must therefore be separate model quantities.

First-hand photographs show slack formed into coils and fixed near the rear glass and conductive support structure. This demonstrates that installed electromagnetic geometry may be created in the field after the nominal design is complete. A nameplate agreeing with a manufacturer datasheet is field-observed corroboration of identity, not an electrical field measurement.

These findings belong in Seed as original engineering interpretation and provenance pointers. Exact manufacturer documents remain external evidence and must be identified by controlled references before implementation values are promoted.
