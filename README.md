# Seed 🌱

Seed is the living knowledge base of the Ventus and GlobalGrid2050 engineering ecosystem.

It is not an application, simulator, standards archive or transcript dump. It is the durable body of original engineering knowledge, reasoning, provenance, governance, capability history and build order from which software, reports, data models, research and future AI systems may grow.

Software changes quickly. Engineering knowledge should endure.

Seed therefore preserves:

- ethical and governance principles;
- engineering logic;
- context and reasoning logic;
- mathematical and physical capabilities;
- topology, object models and schemas;
- provenance and uncertainty;
- validation and regression history;
- authority and migration decisions;
- links across the Ventus repository ecosystem;
- thread handovers and reload documents.

## Core rule

> Seed stores original knowledge, provenance and engineering judgement. It does not reproduce copyrighted standards, books, papers, figures, proprietary drawings or confidential project documents.

External works remain external authorities. Seed may cite or link to them, record a clause or section reference where lawful, and preserve an original engineering interpretation without copying protected expression.

## Engineering order

```text
Ethics and governance
        ↓
Context and evidence
        ↓
Physics and mathematics
        ↓
Geometry and objects
        ↓
Topology and connectivity
        ↓
Computation and validation
        ↓
Reports and interfaces
        ↓
Visualisation
```

The browser is not the engineering authority. The authoritative computation layer must remain testable, reproducible and independent of presentation.

## Axiom Zero

> Seed shall never knowingly forget.

A capability may be superseded, rejected or retired, but its origin, purpose, evidence, defects, replacement and lessons should remain traceable.
