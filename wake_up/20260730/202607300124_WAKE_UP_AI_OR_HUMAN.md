## 5. Copyright, rights and confidentiality boundary

The human expressly instructed: do not violate copyright.

Seed stores original knowledge, original abstractions, provenance, links, engineering judgement and independently expressed explanations.

Seed must not reproduce without a clear lawful basis:

- standards text;
- protected tables or figures;
- books;
- substantial passages from papers;
- proprietary drawings;
- confidential project reports;
- private source material merely because it is technically accessible;
- third-party code without licence compatibility and attribution;
- private repository content in a public Seed file unless publication rights are established.

Access is not permission to republish.

Ownership of a copy is not necessarily ownership of copyright.

Safe forms include:

- original summaries;
- clause and edition references;
- independently derived mathematics;
- original engineering interpretation;
- licence-compatible code migration with obligations preserved;
- generalised lessons that remove confidential identifiers and project-specific expression;
- factual provenance and links.

External sources remain external authorities. Seed points to them and explains their relevance without absorbing protected expression.
