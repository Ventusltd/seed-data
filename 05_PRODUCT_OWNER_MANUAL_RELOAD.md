## Evidence and correction law

The manual reload preserves the principle that evidence promotion has direction.

A manufacturer datasheet is manufacturer-declared evidence.

A photographed nameplate agreeing with that datasheet is field-observed corroboration of equipment identity, not an electrical field measurement.

A published inverter block diagram can narrow an unknown without resolving connector-level internal topology, reverse-current blocking or abnormal backfeed paths.

Derived arithmetic may be exact while its design interpretation remains unresolved. Cold-voltage arithmetic does not automatically define the correct tolerance policy. Summed module short-circuit current may be a useful screen against an inverter operating-current figure without constituting a final compatibility result. String-fusing conclusions remain dependent upon the evidenced parallel neighbourhood, external sources and PCE backfeed.

No future model may promote a correction merely because multiple model summaries repeat it. The corrected source, the superseded claim and the evidence chain must be identifiable.
