## Current known doctrine that must not be forgotten

### Physics before software

Software does not create physical truth.

The implementation follows the physical model, not the reverse.

### Geometry before totals

An entered total cable length is not a geometric model.

Where arrangement affects electrical, electromagnetic, installation or material behaviour, length should be derived from ordered physical objects and routes.

### Objects before equations

Equations should act on explicit engineering objects carrying identity, properties, conditions, provenance and relationships.

### Terminals before connectivity

Electrical circuits should be constructed from defined terminals and valid connections, not inferred from visual proximity.

### Topology before calculation

A plausible number calculated over an invalid or ambiguous circuit is still wrong.

### Formation is distinct from connectivity

Electrically identical circuits may have materially different physical formations and electromagnetic behaviour.

### Units are part of meaning

No calculation without units.

No public bare number whose dimension must be guessed.

### Conditions belong with values

Resistance without temperature and basis is incomplete.

Current, voltage, capacitance and other quantities must preserve the conditions under which they apply.

### Evidence classes must remain distinct

At minimum preserve:

```text
manufacturer_declared
field_measured
public_observation
user_created
derived
generic_example
assumed
external_reference
confidential_source_abstraction
historical_recollection
```

Assumed values must never silently appear as measured or declared facts.

### First-principles physics and compliance are separate

Physical possibility is not automatic compliance.

A standard recommendation is not a law of nature.

Standards cartridges must remain separable from the physics kernel.

### Standards are referenced, not copied

Do not reproduce protected standards wording, tables or figures.

Store original interpretation, edition identity, clause references, required inputs, validity boundaries and implementation receipts.

### Tests before authority

Execution does not create authority.

Canonical promotion requires evidence appropriate to the claim.

### Bugs become knowledge

A corrected defect should leave a regression test, root-cause note and migration record.

### Unknown is lawful

Where evidence is absent, record unknown.

Do not hide it.

Do not guess it green.

### Permanent layers do not depend on temporary consumers

Seed, authoritative ledgers and durable evidence layers must not depend on homepages, dashboards or other temporary presentation systems.

Temporary consumers may read permanent knowledge.

The dependency must not run backwards.

### Build and proof are separate duties

The builder builds.

The verifier verifies.

The human decides.

No assistant should mark its own work as finally authoritative merely because it produced it.

## Copyright and confidentiality boundary

The human explicitly instructed that Seed must not violate copyright.

Seed may preserve:

- original explanations;
- independently derived mathematics;
- original engineering judgement;
- factual observations lawfully obtained;
- public links and identifiers;
- clause references;
- licence-compatible code and ideas with obligations preserved;
- abstracted lessons from confidential work where publication is lawful and project identity is removed.

Seed must not become:

- a copied standards library;
- a copied book or paper archive;
- a public dump of private repositories;
- a repository of proprietary drawings;
- a verbatim confidential report archive;
- an unattributed third-party code collection.

Access is not the same as permission to republish.

Ownership of a physical or digital copy is not automatically ownership of copyright.

For every harvested source, inspect ownership, licence, confidentiality and permitted migration form before reproducing expression or code.

Where rights are uncertain, preserve the link, provenance and original abstraction rather than copying the source.

## Before writing code

Ask:

- Have I read the current doctrine?
- Have I read the repository's own instructions?
- Is this document binding, advisory, historical or superseded?
- Do I know the physical object and scale involved?
- Do I know the source rights?
- Do I know the declared grain and key?
- Do I know which invariant will prove success?
- Am I duplicating a capability that already exists?
- Is the browser being mistaken for the engine?
- Is a report being mistaken for evidence?
- Can another person reproduce this result?
- Will this leave Seed clearer than it was found?

If these questions cannot yet be answered, read first.

Think second.

Build third.
